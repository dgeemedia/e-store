## Tips

- **Keep your promises true.** Say "launching" until the site is live. Do not quote customer numbers or sales figures you do not have yet.
- **Bring the site if they ask.** Showing your test site on a phone makes the pitch concrete, but remember it still has no real products.
- **Follow up in 3 or 4 days** if you do not hear back: "Good day [Name], just checking you received my message."
- **Meet in person for the biggest partners.** Use the message to book a meeting instead of settling terms by chat.
- **The summary is not a contract.** Have your lawyer prepare the real partner agreement (checklist: README section 4c).
