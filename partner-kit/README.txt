PARTNER KIT

Elorge-Partner-Summary.docx   One-page summary to hand or send to each manufacturer. Fill in the yellow blanks first (your fee, payment days, notice periods, contact details).
outreach-messages.md          WhatsApp and email messages to send with it, plus tips.
Product-sheet.csv             Spreadsheet each partner fills in: product names, prices, pack prices, volume discounts, warranty, dispatch time, weight, options, specs and photo file names.
Seller-details-sheet.csv      Spreadsheet for each partner's company details (name, tagline, about, logo).

The two CSV files are copies of the templates in ../import-templates. Delete the EXAMPLE rows, fill in, and save as CSV (UTF-8). When you get them back with the photos, load them with the bulk import (see the main README, section 4b).
